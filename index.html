How to train

Weeks 1–2: 1 working set per strength exercise; if comfortable, build to 2 sets in week 2. Keep 3–4 repetitions in reserve.

Weeks 3–4: 2 sets per strength exercise. Keep 3 repetitions in reserve.

Weeks 5–8: 3 sets for major exercises; 2 sets for accessories. Keep 2–3 reps in reserve.

Weeks 9–11: Maintain sets and gradually increase weight or reps with controlled form. Keep around 2 reps in reserve.

Week 12: Reassess; reduce volume if tired, otherwise continue comfortably.

Start with a weight that feels manageable. When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good technique, increase the weight by the smallest available increment. Rest about 60–120 seconds between sets.

Stop an exercise if it causes sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms. Ask gym staff to check machine setup and technique.
